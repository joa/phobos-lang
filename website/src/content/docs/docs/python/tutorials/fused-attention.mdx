[Tutorial 06](https://triton-lang.org/main/getting-started/tutorials/06-fused-attention.html)
is FlashAttention-2: attention computed a block of queries at a time, with
the softmax carried along the keys so the full score matrix is never stored.
The port runs in fp16 on the tensor cores with the softmax state and the
accumulators in `f32`.

<PythonModule file="python/examples/tutorials/t06_fused_attention.py" />

The batch and the heads are flattened into the rows: `Q`, `K` and `V` are
`[Z H N_CTX, D]`, and a CTA's second grid index, `ctx.block.y`, picks the
head.

- **`attn_fwd`** takes `BR` queries and walks the keys `BC` at a time,
  keeping each row's running maximum and sum, and stores each row's
  log-sum-exp in `L` for the backward pass. Causal attention masks a key past
  the query and stops the walk at the diagonal.
- The backward pass recomputes the probabilities from `L` instead of storing
  them: one kernel for `delta = rowsum(dO * O)`, one for `dK` and `dV` a
  block of keys at a time over the queries, and one for `dQ` a block of
  queries at a time over the keys.

The check is the tutorial's `test_op` at Z 1, H 2, N_CTX 1024, HEAD_DIM 64
and `sm_scale` 0.5, causal and not, forward and backward: the output and the
three gradients against torch, with an absolute tolerance of 1e-2.
